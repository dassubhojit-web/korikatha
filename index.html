Our story

Two words.
One living legacy.

Kori কড়ি

A symbol connected with traditional ornamentation and Bengal’s cultural memory.

Katha কথা

A story—spoken, inherited, remembered and shared from one generation to the next.

✦

Together, KoriKatha represents the stories carried by every weave, motif and handcrafted detail.

We preserve Bengali craftsmanship while presenting it in a fresh, contemporary way—connecting today’s generation with the artisans and traditions that make Bengal’s heritage so special.
